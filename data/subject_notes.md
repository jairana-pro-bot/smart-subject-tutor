5. Common Grammar Traps to Avoid
5.1 Stative Verbs
Verbs describing feelings, senses, possession, or mental states (know, believe, love, hate, own, understand, seem) are rarely used in Continuous tenses.

Incorrect: I am knowing the answer.

Correct: I know the answer.

5.2 Past Time with Present Perfect
Never use a specific past time marker (yesterday, in 2022, an hour ago) with Present Perfect. Use Simple Past instead.

Incorrect: I have met him yesterday.

Correct: I met him yesterday.

5.3 Double Future in Conditionals
In sentences starting with if, when, unless, or until, use Simple Present instead of Simple Future in the condition clause.

Incorrect: If it will rain, we will stay inside.

Correct: If it rains, we will stay inside.
